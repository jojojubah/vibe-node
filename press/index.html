About VibeNode

Copy as-is, or trim to fit.

One line

VibeNode is an independent UK software studio that makes focused, fairly priced apps and games for Apple platforms and beyond.

Paragraph

VibeNode is an independent software studio based in London. Founded in 2026, it designs and ships apps, games and AI features for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows, Android and the web. Its apps include Learn Liquid Glass, a hands-on way for developers to learn Apple’s Liquid Glass design in SwiftUI, Roulette Play, a free roulette game played with play chips only, and Dotz & Boxz, the classic dots and boxes game with real, wobbly ropes. VibeNode builds software around a simple idea: useful, well-made technology at fair prices, without unnecessary subscriptions, ads or tracking.

Apps

Learn Liquid Glass

Education · iPhone, iPad and Mac · Free

Learn Liquid Glass teaches Apple’s Liquid Glass design in SwiftUI by doing. It has more than 50 guided lessons and over 200 working examples that developers can adjust live and copy the Swift code from. Optional on-device help from Apple Intelligence explains where glass belongs. No account, no tracking, and it works offline. Requires iOS 26, iPadOS 26 or macOS 26.

Roulette Play: Casino Game

Games · iPhone, iPad and Apple Vision Pro · Free · 18+

Roulette Play is classic roulette for iPhone and iPad, with a full touch table, realistic wheel spins and a choice of European or American wheel. Players earn Casino Points to unlock table themes and ball finishes, and collect 16 Game Center achievements. It uses play chips only: there is no real-money gambling, cash-out or prizes. Available in eight languages. Formerly called Vroulette.

Dotz & Boxz

Games · iPhone and iPad · Free · 4+

Dotz & Boxz is the classic pencil-and-paper game of dots and boxes, rebuilt with real, physics-driven ropes that twang into place and sway when you tilt the device. Players take on Robo, a friendly computer opponent, on three levels or play a friend on one device, earn coins by playing, and spend them on rainbow ropes, golden boards and new looks. It has 14 Game Center achievements, Daily Rewards and quests, no ads and no tracking, and works offline. An optional Animal Friends Pack adds six animal characters.
