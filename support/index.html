Support

For Learn Liquid Glass support, bug reports, or feedback, contact contact@vibenode.co.uk.

We are happy to help with Learn Liquid Glass questions, app issues, App Store installation questions, and feedback about lessons or examples.

When you contact us, please include your device model, OS version, app version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are useful if you are reporting a visual issue.

Email: contact@vibenode.co.uk

We aim to respond to support messages within two business days.
