Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 10, 2026

Vibe Node Ltd respects your privacy. This policy covers the Learn Liquid Glass app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac, and the Vibe Node website at vibenode.co.uk.

Learn Liquid Glass does not use tracking, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, third-party tracking SDKs, or third-party advertising networks. We do not collect personal data from the app.

The app stores progress, bookmarks, settings, and theme preferences locally on your device using Apple system storage such as SwiftData and UserDefaults. This local app data is not sent to Vibe Node Ltd.

Liquid AI features, when available, use on-device Apple Intelligence and Foundation Models. Code snippets and natural language prompts are processed on your device and are not sent to Vibe Node Ltd servers or external AI providers by Learn Liquid Glass.

The Vibe Node website may process information you submit directly, such as support emails, and stores cookie consent preferences. We use this information only to respond to your request, maintain the website, and meet legal obligations.
