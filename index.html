Vroulette

Classic roulette for iPhone and iPad. A full casino table, realistic wheel spins, and your choice of European or American wheel — played with play chips only.

The table

A full table, built for touch

Place chips on numbers, splits, corners, and every outside bet. Announced bets like Voisins and Tiers lay out their chips for you.

Results

Every winning number, celebrated

The wheel sweeps in close for each spin, and the winning number stays marked on the table so you can see exactly what paid.

  • European or American

    Play the single-zero European wheel or the double-zero American wheel, each with its own table.

  • Free play chips

    Top up with free play chips every two hours. Optional chip packs are also available.

  • Game Center

    Play with your Game Center profile and find Vroulette alongside your other games.

  • Private by design

    No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your balance and settings stay on your device.

Vroulette is simulated roulette for entertainment, intended for adults aged 18 and over. It uses play chips only: there is no real-money gambling, cash-out, or prizes. Optional chip packs are in-app purchases processed by Apple; play chips have no cash value, can’t be withdrawn or exchanged, and purchases never change the odds.
