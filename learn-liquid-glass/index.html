What you’ll learn

Lessons are organised by the job you’re doing, so you can jump to what your app needs today.

  • Glass foundations — where Liquid Glass belongs on the control layer, and where standard materials suit the content layer.
  • Controls — buttons, toggles, sliders and menus with glass effects and custom shapes.
  • Navigation and system chrome — toolbars, tab bars, sidebars, and bottom accessories.
  • Interaction and motion — morphing, merging glass shapes, and scroll edge effects.
  • Platform APIs — the SwiftUI modifiers behind it all, with the OS versions each one needs.
  • Shipping quality — contrast, accessibility, and reduced transparency, so glass stays readable.

Questions

What is Learn Liquid Glass?

An app for learning Apple’s Liquid Glass design language in SwiftUI. It has more than 50 guided lessons and over 200 working examples you can open, adjust with live controls, and copy the Swift code from.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, iPad and Mac, on iOS 26, iPadOS 26 or macOS 26 or later. It includes adaptive layouts for iPhone Duo.

How much does it cost?

It’s free on the App Store, with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads.

Does it need an account or collect data?

No. There’s no account and no tracking. Progress, bookmarks and settings stay on your device, and the app works offline.

How does the AI help work?

Where Apple Intelligence is available, Liquid AI answers questions about where Liquid Glass belongs and suggests Swift code. It runs on-device, so your prompts and code are never sent to a server.

Can I use the example code in my own apps?

Yes. Every example shows the Swift behind it, ready to paste into your own project, along with the frameworks it uses and the OS versions it needs.
