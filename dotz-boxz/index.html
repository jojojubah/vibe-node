Questions

Is Dotz & Boxz free?

Yes. It’s free to download, with no ads. Coins are earned by playing only; they can’t be bought and have no cash value. The only purchase is the optional Animal Friends Pack (£1.99, one time), kept behind a grown-up check.

How do you play dots and boxes?

Take turns tying a rope between two neighbouring pegs. Close the fourth side of a box and it’s yours, and you get another go. When every rope is tied, the player with the most boxes wins.

Can I play with a friend?

Yes. Choose With a friend to pass one iPhone or iPad between two players, or play Robo, the friendly computer opponent, on Easy, Normal or Hard.

Is it suitable for children?

It’s rated 4+. There are no ads, no chat, no accounts and no tracking, and the one in-app purchase sits behind a grown-up multiplication check followed by Apple’s own purchase sheet.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything works offline. If you use iCloud, your coins and progress sync between your own devices through Apple.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or iPadOS 26 or later.
