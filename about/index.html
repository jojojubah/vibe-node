About VibeNode

Good software. Fairly built.

There’s a lot of noise in technology.

Endless subscriptions. Aggressive upsells. Ads everywhere. Features locked away for no good reason. Products designed around how much they can extract from you rather than how useful they can be.

We want VibeNode to be different.

We believe technology, and especially AI, should make great software more accessible to everyone. As the tools available to builders become more powerful, we think some of that value should be passed on to the people using what we create.

That means building useful, well-made software at fair prices. Avoiding unnecessary subscriptions. Keeping advertising out where we can. Being straightforward about what something costs and what you actually get for your money.

We’re a small independent company, and we want to build the kind of technology we’d be happy to pay for ourselves.

No tricks. Just good software, built responsibly.
